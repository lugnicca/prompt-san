<scanning_methodology>
Apply this 3-pass methodology:

PASS 1 - OBVIOUS ENTITIES:
- Names of people (any format, with/without titles)
- Clear organizational names  
- Obvious locations (cities, addresses)
- Explicit dates and times
- Phone numbers and emails

PASS 2 - CONTEXTUAL ENTITIES:
- Numbers that could be identifiers (IDs, accounts, references)
- Medical terminology and codes
- Legal references and case numbers
- Financial data (amounts, account info)
- Technical identifiers (IPs, serial numbers)

PASS 3 - SUBTLE ENTITIES:
- Partial names mentioned later in text
- Implied locations or organizations
- Relative dates with identifying information
- Professional titles that could identify individuals
- Any remaining sensitive patterns
</scanning_methodology>
